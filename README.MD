# 🚀 AI-Enabled Career Guidance Platform

> An intelligent AI-powered platform that helps students and job seekers discover suitable career paths, identify skill gaps, find learning opportunities, improve resumes, prepare for interviews, and explore internship, job, and government employment opportunities.

---

## 📌 Overview

Choosing the right career can be difficult for students and job seekers because of limited awareness, rapidly changing industry requirements, lack of personalized guidance, and difficulty identifying the skills required for specific careers.

The **AI-Enabled Career Guidance Platform** addresses this problem by providing personalized, data-driven career guidance through Artificial Intelligence.

The platform analyzes a user's:

- Academic background
- Skills
- Interests
- Career preferences
- Experience
- Existing resume
- Career goals

Based on this information, the system provides personalized recommendations for suitable career paths, required skills, courses, certifications, internships, jobs, government opportunities, resume improvements, and interview preparation.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many students choose careers without having sufficient information about:

- Which career is suitable for their skills and interests
- What skills are required for a particular career
- Which courses or certifications they should pursue
- Where to find relevant internships and jobs
- How to improve their resume
- How to prepare for interviews
- What government employment opportunities are available

Traditional career counselling is often expensive, generalized, or inaccessible.

There is a need for an **accessible, personalized, and intelligent career guidance system** that can provide recommendations based on an individual's profile.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Our platform works as an **AI-powered personal career assistant**.

The user creates a profile and provides information such as education, skills, interests, experience, and career goals.

The AI analyzes the profile and generates personalized recommendations.

### Core capabilities

1. 👤 Student Profile Analysis
2. 🎯 Career Path Recommendation
3. 📊 Skill Gap Analysis
4. 📚 Course & Certification Recommendations
5. 💼 Internship & Job Matching
6. 📄 AI Resume Improvement
7. 🎤 AI Mock Interviews
8. 🏛️ Government Job Recommendations
9. 📈 Personalized Career Roadmap
10. 🤖 AI Career Assistant
